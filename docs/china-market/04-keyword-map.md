# 04 · Chinese Keyword Map

**Method.** Seed terms from the brief were expanded with (a) Simplified-Chinese morphological variants (调查/侦探/核实/核查/查询/寻人/找人), (b) Thailand place modifiers (泰国/曼谷/芭提雅/普吉/清迈/春武里/苏梅), (c) intent modifiers (费用/价格/多少钱/怎么/靠谱/公司/机构/推荐/流程/合法), (d) the segment vocabulary in the brief. Volumes are **not** stated: no Baidu Index / Google Keyword Planner (zh) export exists in the repo. Column "Validate" lists the tool to confirm demand before writing further content. **Keywords implying illegal data access were deliberately excluded** (e.g. 查开房记录, 查通话记录, 泰国手机定位, 查银行流水, 查身份证信息) and must never be targeted or auto-generated by the article pipeline.

## Content gaps observed in the Chinese market generally

1. Almost no Chinese content explains **what a Thai investigator can and cannot legally obtain** — strongest trust-builder.
2. Little content on **remote-client workflow** (how to brief, pay, receive evidence from abroad).
3. Due-diligence content for Chinese SMEs entering Thailand is mostly law-firm marketing; an investigator's "physical verification" angle is unoccupied.
4. No Chinese-language anonymised case formats with timelines/deliverables.

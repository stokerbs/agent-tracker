Scam calls and messages reach almost everyone in Thailand. When an unknown number keeps calling, or someone you met online gives you only a phone number and a social-media handle, you want to know who is behind it before you reply, transfer money or meet. Detective Pulse offers a **phone number check** built on the lawful open-source methods of our [cyber investigation](/en/cyber-investigation) team.

## What we can check

- Whether the number appears in public scam reports, call-centre-fraud databases or online complaints
- Which social-media accounts, online shops or public listings the number is openly attached to, and whether they match what the person told you
- Contact patterns that match known fraud scripts (impersonating government agencies, banks, couriers) and how to respond
- If you have already been defrauded or harassed from the number, we organise the evidence you hold (screenshots, transfer slips, chat logs) into a file suitable for a police report and legal action
- For **your own** SIM or number: how to verify registration status and unusual usage with your carrier, and how to secure the accounts linked to it

## What we do not do

- We do not obtain another person's call history, messages or SIM-registration data from a telecom operator — that data is legally protected and available only to the number's owner or to officials under a court order
- We do not locate another person's phone, intercept calls, or install monitoring software
- We do not run "reverse lookups" against leaked or back-door databases; anyone selling that is breaking the law, and the results are usually wrong

## When to use this service

- A number keeps calling or sends links, and you want to know whether it is a scam before you engage
- You met someone online and want to verify their identity before sending money, investing or travelling to meet them (see also our [background check](/en/background-check))
- You have been scammed and need the number and accounts investigated to help [locate the person](/en/find-missing-person) and support a police complaint
